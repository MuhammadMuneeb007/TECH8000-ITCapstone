Listen beyond the request

A requested feature is not automatically the real need. Ask what outcome the client expects.

Observe the current workflow

What people actually do may be different from what they describe in a meeting.

Separate symptoms from causes

Slow reports may be a symptom. Duplicate entry, poor ownership, or fragmented data may be the cause.
